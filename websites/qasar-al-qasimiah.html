About

Pakistani cooking, the everyday kind

A straightforward neighbourhood kitchen in Al Nad — no frills, just the dishes people grew up eating.

Qasar Al Qasimiah is a Pakistani restaurant on Abdullah Bin Suhail Street in Al Nad, Sharjah, serving Pakistani and Indian food for dine-in, takeaway and delivery. The menu runs from biryani, karahi and tandoor barbecue to old-fashioned favourites like nihari, haleem and daal chawal, plus a short line-up of fast food and desserts.

It's a casual, no-frills spot rather than a fine-dining one — the kind of place regulars call ahead for a takeaway order on their way home, or settle into for a late dinner after everything else nearby has closed. The kitchen stays open into the early hours, which has made it a regular stop for shift workers and late-night diners in the area.
