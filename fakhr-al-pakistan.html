Welcome to Our Table

Located in Muwaileh Commercial, Fakhr Al Pakistan is a family-operated restaurant dedicated to the vibrant, robust flavors of traditional Pakistani cuisine. From hearty breakfasts of Halwa Puri to late-night Shinwari Karahi, our kitchen is open 24 hours a day to serve you.

We pride ourselves on using fresh ingredients, preparing our handis and karahis live to order, and baking our tandoori breads throughout the day to ensure every meal feels like home.
