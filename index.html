FAQ

How long does a website take?
That depends on the size and requirements of the website. I'll give you an estimated timeline after understanding what you need.
Can you make the website mobile-friendly?
Yes. Websites are designed to work across phones, tablets and desktops.
Can you update my website later?
Yes. Ongoing updates and maintenance can be provided.
Do I need to provide everything?
No. You can provide whatever information, photos, menus and branding you already have, and I'll help organize it into the website.
Can you connect WhatsApp?
Yes. WhatsApp can be integrated so customers can contact your business directly.
Do you provide hosting?
Hosting solutions can be discussed and set up depending on the specific requirements of your project.
